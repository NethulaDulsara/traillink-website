★ Published Academic Research Paper • IEEE Conference Standard (6 Pages)

Offline-First Location Sharing, Mapping, and Priority-Based Bounded SOS Routing in Hybrid Mobile Ad-Hoc Networks: The TrailLink Platform

Lead Author: Nethula Dulsara (IT22544490)
Affiliation: Department of IT, Faculty of Computing, SLIIT
Supervisors: Mr. Vishan Jayasinghearachchi & Ms. Sasini Hathurusinghe
Status: Completed • Downloadable PDF

Formal empirical validation of unprivileged user-space location tracking, dynamic temporal freshness decay, loop-free TTL-governed distress broadcasting, and atomic SQLite-to-MongoDB reconciliation across 750 physical trials (Open Area, Light Forest, Concrete Classrooms). Achieving 99.5% PDR at 20m and sub-850ms multi-wall emergency alert latency.

★ Final Unified Master Dissertation • SLIIT CDAP 2026 Group: R26-IT-135 • 121 Pages

TRAILLINK: Hybrid Communication & Safety System — Main Group Research Thesis (Draft)

Research Group: R26-IT-135
Scope: 121 Pages • 12 Chapters • Unified System Integration
Research Team: Thanujana D O, Pabodhana D M D I, Dulsara H G N, Vitharana G G
Supervisors: Mr. Vishan Jayasinghearachchi & Ms. Sashini Hathurusinghe
Department: Dept. of IT, Faculty of Computing, SLIIT • Malabe, Sri Lanka
Status: Completed Draft • Downloadable PDF

The comprehensive group master dissertation synthesizing all four research subsystems: (1) Connectivity, Mesh Intelligence & Network Compass, (2) Delay-Tolerant Communication Core Engine, (3) Voice and Push-to-Talk (PTT) Subsystem, and (4) Offline Geospatial Vector Mapping & Emergency SOS Protocol. Validated on a 5-node testbed across 750 physical trials (Open Trail, Forest Canopy, Multi-Wall Classrooms), achieving 99.4% 1-hop PDR, 94.2% 4-hop PDR, 0.00% duplicate propagation, and <850ms SOS latency on unmodified consumer Android smartphones.
