Project Assessments & Academic Milestones

Chronological record of all evaluated academic milestones, submission deadlines, marks allocations, and assessment criteria set by the SLIIT CDAP evaluation committee.

Milestone 01
📅 March 2026
✓ Completed & Evaluated

Project Proposal Defense

The formal inception of the research project. Involves formulation of the research problem, preliminary literature review of mobile ad-hoc networking and satellite safety devices, scoping of the four individual subsystems, and feasibility analysis on unrooted Android devices.

Key Deliverables & Evaluation Criteria:
  • Project Charter document specifying scope and team responsibilities.
  • Formal Project Proposal Report with PRISMA literature search scope.
  • Official slide presentation: R26-IT-135_Proposal Presentation.pptx.
  • Verification of zero-root, unprivileged execution feasibility.
Milestone 02
📅 May 2026
✓ Completed & Evaluated

Progress Presentation 1 (PP1)

Evaluation of architectural design, system requirements (MoSCoW analysis), and proof-of-concept prototypes for P2P connection establishment. Demonstrated initial unprivileged Bluetooth Low Energy discovery and dynamic Wi-Fi Direct socket promotion using Google Nearby Connections.

Key Deliverables & Evaluation Criteria:
  • Detailed software requirements specification (Functional & Non-Functional).
  • Official slide presentation: R26-IT-135_Progress Presentation 1.pptx.
  • DDL schemas for embedded SQLite databases with Write-Ahead Logging (WAL).
  • Functional P2P demonstration of peer discovery and raw byte socket handshake.
Milestone 03
📅 July 2026
✓ Completed & Evaluated

Progress Presentation 2 (PP2)

Evaluation of core subsystem implementation (over 50% implementation threshold). Demonstrated offline MBTiles vector map rendering, spatio-temporal coordinate freshness decay badges, out-of-order emergency SOS packet dispatching, and broadcast storm suppression via idempotent ledgers.

Key Deliverables & Evaluation Criteria:
  • Integrated demonstration of offline location sharing across two handsets.
  • Official slide presentation: R26-IT-135 .pptx.
  • Emergency SOS preemption over background conversational queues.
  • Voice radio jitter buffer demonstration and link quality scoring algorithm.
Milestone 04
📅 September - October 2026
✓ Completed & Submitted

Final Assessment & Research Thesis Submission

The comprehensive culmination of the research project. Encompasses the full empirical benchmarking of 750 physical trials across Open Area, Forest Canopy, and Multi-Wall Classroom environments, complete software system integration, and submission of the peer-reviewed research paper and group final thesis document.

Key Deliverables & Evaluation Criteria:
  • Submission of Draft Group Research Thesis (121 Pages, 12 Chapters, 4 Subsystems).
  • Peer-reviewed Research Paper manuscript (IEEE conference format, 6 pages).
  • Final Group Comprehensive Research Report integrating all 4 subsystems (121 Pages).
  • Complete source code repository with automated unit, integration, and UI tests.

Milestone 05
📅 October 2026
⏳ Scheduled & Active

Website Viva & Final Presentation Defense

The final oral defense conducted before the SLIIT Faculty of Computing examination board. Evaluates the candidate's mastery of the research project, live demonstration of the unprivileged offline mesh application, cross-component data bus synchronization, and evaluation of this academic research information website.

Key Deliverables & Evaluation Criteria:
  • Live physical demonstration on two Google Pixel 7 handsets in disconnected mode.
  • Academic Research Information Website evaluation (<20MB course web package).
  • Individual defense of contributions, mathematical models, and latency findings.
  • Viva examination Q&A addressing commercialization, limitations, and future directions.
